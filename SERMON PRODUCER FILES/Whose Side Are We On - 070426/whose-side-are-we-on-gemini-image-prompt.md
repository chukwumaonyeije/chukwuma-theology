# Hero Image Prompt (Gemini / Imagen)

## Prompt

A cinematic, fine-art biblical realism illustration for a theology essay titled "Whose Side Are We On?", based on Joshua 5:13-15. Wide 16:9 editorial composition, painterly realism blended with photographic detail, restrained and reverent in tone.

Scene: on a desert plain at dusk just outside the walls of ancient Jericho, Joshua — a rugged Israelite military commander in worn leather and bronze armor — stands frozen mid-step, one sandal just slipping loose from his foot as he begins to kneel. Before him stands the Commander of the LORD's host: a tall, radiant, unmistakably divine figure holding a drawn sword lowered at rest, not raised in threat. Warm, soft golden light seems to emanate from the Commander himself rather than any visible sun, illuminating only the small patch of ground between the two figures while the wider landscape fades into cool blue-gray desert dusk. Jericho's massive stone walls are visible in the soft-focus distance, present but not the focus of the frame.

Mood: sacred confrontation resolving into surrender, not combat — the hush of a soldier realizing he is not the one in command. Avoid any sense of battle, violence, or triumphalism.

Style: premium fine-art realism, similar to a serious history painting crossed with modern cinematic photography — think Rembrandt lighting meets contemporary editorial illustration. Muted, sophisticated color palette (deep indigo dusk, warm amber glow, desert ochre and stone gray). No cartoonish rendering, no glowing CGI-angel wings, no fantasy-game aesthetic, no bright saturated colors, no modern clothing, flags, or political imagery, no crowds — only the two figures. Leave clean negative space in the upper third of the frame for a title overlay. No text or lettering rendered in the image itself.

## Negative Prompt

modern flags, political signs, contemporary clothing, battle carnage, violence, glowing wings, fantasy angel tropes, action-movie poster staging, cartoon style, oversaturated colors, cluttered composition, embedded text or lettering, crowd scenes, extra figures

## Aspect Ratio

16:9 (landscape hero banner)
