# Hero Image Prompt

## Primary Prompt

Create a cinematic editorial hero image for a theological essay titled **"Whose Side Are We On?"** based on **Joshua 5:13-15**.

Show the moment Joshua looks up near Jericho and sees the Commander of the LORD's host standing before him, sword drawn and lowered, not raised in threat but held at rest with quiet, absolute authority. Joshua is on the ground in front of Him, already dropping to his knees, one sandal just coming loose from his foot. The setting is the edge of a moonlit or last-light desert plain outside Jericho's walls, which loom soft and distant in the background - present, but not the visual subject of the frame.

The Commander should read as unmistakably divine without literal halo cliches: a still, radiant authority in His bearing, warm light seeming to originate from His presence rather than from any obvious external source, illuminating the patch of ground beneath Him and Joshua while the wider landscape stays in deep dusk tones. The mood is confrontation resolving into surrender - not violence, not a battle scene, but the hush right before an army commander realizes he is not the one in command.

Visual language should feel premium, restrained, and theologically weighty - fine-art biblical realism, closer to a serious history painting than a movie poster. Avoid triumphalism, glowing CGI-angel spectacle, or battle-epic bombast. This is a scene of holy confrontation and surrender, not conquest.

Composition: wide cinematic crop with negative space in the upper third for title text, the Commander placed slightly off-center holding visual weight, Joshua smaller in frame and lower, sandal and drawn sword both visible but secondary to the exchange of gazes between the two figures. Lighting should feel like holy ground made visible - a single warm source cutting through cool desert dusk.

## Style Keywords

- cinematic
- fine-art biblical realism
- reverent
- editorial
- desert dusk
- sacred confrontation
- quiet authority
- theological gravitas
- restrained radiance

## Negative Prompt

- no modern flags, political signage, or contemporary clothing
- no battle carnage or explicit violence
- no glowing CGI wings or fantasy-angel tropes
- no triumphant action-movie poster staging
- no cartoonish or overly stylized rendering
- no bright saturated colors
- no cluttered composition
- no obvious text embedded in image
- no crowd scenes - only the two figures

## Brand Fit Check

- The image should feel recognizably Chukwuma Theology: serious, pastoral, contemporary in emotional intelligence, and biblically grounded.
- It should keep the scene inside the biblical narrative rather than illustrating the modern political framing from the essay's hook - the theology, not the headlines, should carry the visual.
- It should leave room for a title treatment without losing the central moment: a drawn sword, a lowered sandal, and a King mistaken for a soldier.
