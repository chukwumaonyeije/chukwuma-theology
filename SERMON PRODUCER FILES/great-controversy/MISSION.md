# Mission: The Great Controversy

## Why
To develop a deep, working understanding of *The Great Controversy* by Ellen G. White that can be translated for people outside Seventh-day Adventism — explaining this foundational Adventist framework in terms anyone can grasp, without assuming prior SDA vocabulary or doctrine.

## Success looks like
- Able to explain the "great controversy theme" clearly to a non-SDA Christian or secular listener
- Able to trace the book's historical narrative from the early church through the Reformation to the end times
- Able to articulate the distinctives of Adventist prophetic interpretation (the sanctuary, 1844, the Sabbath) in accessible, non-insider language
- Able to use GC insights in preaching, writing, and conversation with people of varied theological backgrounds

## Reading approach
Using lessons to cover and distill the content — supplemented by direct reading of the text as needed.

## Prior knowledge
Established Adventist background; familiar with SDA doctrine and Ellen White's broader corpus. The goal here is *accessible explanation outward*, not personal doctrinal introduction.

## Constraints
- Lessons should be completable in 10–15 minutes
- Start with the big picture before moving chapter-by-chapter

## Out of scope
- Deep apologetics debates about 1844 (for now)
- Comparing GC with non-Adventist eschatological systems in technical detail
