# GC Framework Understood as Cosmic Courtroom Over God's Character

Chukwuma understands the great controversy as a cosmic conflict — not primarily about winning a battle, but about answering Satan's charges against the character and government of God. He can place the book's four-part historical arc (Dark Ages → Reformation → Advent Movement → Final Conflict) and is approaching the study as a translation task: explaining Adventist insider theology in terms accessible to non-SDA Christians and secular readers.

**Implications:** Future lessons can move directly to the theological specifics — Lucifer's original charge, the Reformation chapters, the sanctuary doctrine — without spending time establishing the basic GC framework. The translation lens should be held consistently: always ask how each chapter's claims would land for someone outside the SDA tradition.
