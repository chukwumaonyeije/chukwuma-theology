# The Great Controversy — Resources

## Primary Sources

- [*The Great Controversy* — Ellen G. White (egwwritings.org)](https://egwwritings.org/book/b132)
  The text itself. All lessons cite chapter and paragraph numbers from this edition. Free, searchable, authoritative.

- [Ellen White Writings (egwwritings.org)](https://egwwritings.org)
  Full EGW corpus. Use for cross-referencing GC themes in *Desire of Ages*, *Patriarchs and Prophets*, *The Acts of the Apostles*.

## Historical & Theological Reference

- [*Daniel and the Revelation* — Uriah Smith (egwwritings.org)](https://egwwritings.org/book/b137)
  Classic SDA exposition of the prophetic framework underlying Part III–IV of the GC.

- [Adventist Biblical Research Institute (biblicalresearch.gc.adventist.org)](https://biblicalresearch.gc.adventist.org)
  Peer-reviewed SDA scholarship. High-trust for doctrinal and historical questions.

- [Andrews University Seminary](https://www.andrews.edu/sem/)
  Academic SDA scholarship. Use for deep historical and prophetic questions.

- [Ellen White Estate](https://ellenwhite.org)
  Authoritative source for EGW research and context questions.

## Historical Background (Secular, High-Trust)

- *The Reformation* — Owen Chadwick (Oxford University Press)
  Context for Part I–II of the GC. Secular historian; reliable on facts about Wycliffe, Huss, Luther, Calvin.

- *Church History in Plain Language* — Bruce Shelley
  Accessible overview of the full arc of Christian history. Useful for non-SDA context.

## For Non-SDA Audiences (Bridging Resources)

- [*The Great Controversy* Introduction — Ellen White Estate](https://ellenwhite.org/content/file/great-controversy-introduction)
  Ellen White's own introduction explaining the book's scope and purpose.

- [Adventist Review (adventistreview.org)](https://www.adventistreview.org)
  Pastoral and contemporary application articles.

- [Ministry Magazine (ministrymagazine.org)](https://www.ministrymagazine.org)
  Pastoral preaching and theological reflection for ministers.
