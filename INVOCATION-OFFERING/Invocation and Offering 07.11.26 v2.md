# Invocation and Offering — July 11, 2026
### Communion Sabbath — Ephesians 2:14–19

---

## INVOCATION

Our Loving Heavenly Father,

On this sacred Sabbath morning, we enter Your presence with gratitude, for You have called us away from the noise and burdens of the week and gathered us into the household of faith. We have not come as strangers seeking admission, but as sons and daughters welcomed by grace, redeemed by Jesus, and united through one Spirit to You.

Your Word declares, “For He Himself is our peace, who has made both one, and has broken down the middle wall of separation.” Lord, Christ is not merely the One who speaks peace to us—He Himself is our peace. Where sin created distance, His cross has brought us near. Where pride and prejudice divided us, His love has made one new family.

As we worship You today, remove from our hearts every barrier that does not belong in Your house. Forgive the resentments we have protected, the judgments we have quietly carried, and the ways we have treated members of Your family as though they were strangers. By Your Holy Spirit, make us a people in whom the reconciling power of the gospel can be seen.

As we approach the Communion table, turn our eyes again toward Jesus. In the bread, help us remember His body given for us. In the cup, help us remember His blood poured out to bring us near. May this sacred service renew our love for Christ and restore our fellowship with one another.

We lift before You those who feel far away today—the grieving, the lonely, the discouraged, the sick, and those who wonder whether there is still a place for them among Your people. Draw them close, gracious Father, and let this congregation become a living witness that in Christ there are no unwanted guests and no forgotten members of the household.

Bless those who will lead us, those who will minister through music and Scripture, and those who will serve at Your table. Let every word spoken and every act of worship direct us toward Jesus. And as we rest in the gift of this Sabbath, prepare us for the eternal fellowship of Your kingdom, when Your redeemed family will gather at one table and separation will be no more.

In the name of Jesus Christ, our peace, our Savior, and our soon-coming King, we pray. Amen.

## CALL FOR OFFERING

It is time for our tithes and offerings.

There is something deeply meaningful about a table with an extra place prepared. An empty chair can say, before a single word is spoken, “We are expecting someone. There is room here. You are welcome.” Hospitality begins long before the guest arrives. Someone provides the meal, prepares the room, and makes certain that the person who comes through the door will not be treated as a stranger.

On this Communion Sabbath, we gather around a table prepared for us by Christ. None of us earned our place here. We were not born with a claim upon it, and we could not purchase admission. Yet Jesus made room for us at an immeasurable cost—His own body given and His own blood poured out.

Paul writes in Ephesians 2:17–19: “And He came and preached peace to you who were afar off and to those who were near. For through Him we both have access by one Spirit to the Father. Now, therefore, you are no longer strangers and foreigners, but fellow citizens with the saints and members of the household of God.”

That is the gospel: strangers become family. Those who were far away are brought near. Those who had no standing are given a home in the household of God.

Our giving participates in that welcome. Through faithful tithes and generous offerings, the gospel travels beyond the walls of this sanctuary. Congregations are strengthened, children are taught, ministries serve their communities, and people who believe they are far from God hear that Christ has prepared a place for them. We do not give to purchase a seat at God’s table. We give because Jesus has already welcomed us to it.

Today, as we return our tithes and bring our offerings, let every gift become a declaration: by the grace of God, there is still room in His house. There is still a place for the stranger. There is still an invitation for the one who is far away.

### PRAYER FOR TITHES AND OFFERINGS

Gracious Heavenly Father, thank You for bringing us near through Jesus Christ and making us members of Your household. Receive these tithes and offerings as acts of gratitude from hearts that have been welcomed by grace. Bless every giver, and use every gift to extend the invitation of the gospel to those who still feel distant, forgotten, or alone. May Your church faithfully prepare a place for them until the day Your whole redeemed family gathers in Your kingdom. In Jesus’ name, we pray. Amen.

Thank you for your generous hearts. God bless you all.

## COMMUNION PRAYERS

### Prayer for the Bread

Lord Jesus, we thank You for this bread, a symbol of Your body given for us. You bore in Your flesh the hostility of sin so that those who were divided might be made one. As we partake, deepen our gratitude for Your sacrifice and remove from us every pride and resentment that wounds the fellowship of Your body. Make us one in You, faithful members of the household You purchased at the cross. In Jesus’ name, Amen.

### Prayer for the Cup

Our Heavenly Father, we thank You for this cup, a symbol of the blood of Jesus poured out for our salvation. Through His blood, we who were far away have been brought near and given access to You. As we partake, cleanse us, renew Your covenant within us, and fill us with the peace Christ alone can give. Keep us faithful until the day we drink with Him anew in Your everlasting kingdom. In Jesus’ name, Amen.
