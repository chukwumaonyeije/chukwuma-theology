## Executive Summary
This outline distills the Demas/Luke contrast into a direct call for endurance in a world that rewards partial faithfulness and public success. It presents spiritual collapse as a slow erosion of affection and treats Luke as the model of durable, ordinary fidelity. The note is best read as a portable discipleship application of the broader [[two-men-one-gospel|Two Men, One Gospel]] sermon family.

## Concepts
- "Almost faithful" is the tragedy the outline is trying to name.
- Demas represents insider drift, not outsider hostility.
- Luke represents steady presence, vocational faithfulness, and durable attachment to Christ.
- Revelation 2:4-5 and 1 John 2:15 sharpen the moral center of the outline.

- The outline is compact enough to be reused for devotionals, small groups, or sermon conclusion material.
