## Executive Summary
This sermon cluster presents 1 Kings 19 as a word for people who are not merely tired but emptied out after pressure, victory, and fear. Its core claim is that God meets Elijah at empty, not after Elijah has already recovered himself. The message keeps rest, silence, remnant theology, and renewed commission together so that divine comfort never becomes detached from divine calling.

## Concepts
- Chapter 19 is unintelligible without chapter 18, because collapse follows immediately after Carmel.
- God's first ministry to Elijah is physical and gentle before it becomes verbal and corrective.
- The still small voice is not weakness; it is the mode by which God retrains perception and relocates Elijah inside a truer story.
- Sabbath, creaturely limits, and God's preservation of the remnant all reinforce the sermon's theology of rest and continuation.

## Insights
- "The tank is not low. It's empty." is the most memorable diagnostic phrase in the material and names the sermon audience with precision.
