## Executive Summary
This cluster proposes a staged sermon-building method that uses AI as an accelerant for disciplined theological work rather than as a substitute for judgment. The sequence is consistent across files: immerse in the biblical text, excavate the theology, build the sermonic burden, and only then draft and refine for proclamation. Although the language borrows from Demis Hassabis and deep-work frameworks, the method is ultimately ordered toward hearing God clearly enough to preach 1 Kings 19 faithfully.

## Concepts
- Read the text before the notes, and let canonical context set the boundaries of interpretation.
- Separate the workflow into immersion, theological excavation, sermon architecture, drafting, and refinement.
- Use AI as a cognitive exoskeleton for synthesis, comparison, and structure, but keep conviction, theological judgment, and pastoral tone in human hands.
- Keep the primary burden on the character of God in 1 Kings 19, with mental-health material functioning as bridge rather than center.
