## Primary Prompt (descriptive)

Create a cinematic editorial hero image for a theological essay titled **"Not Far From the Kingdom"** based on **Mark 12:28-34**.

Show a first-century Jewish scribe standing at a stone threshold inside the Jerusalem temple complex — the exact boundary line between an outer courtyard and an inner one. One sandaled foot rests on the worn threshold stone, but he has not crossed it. In his hand, held loosely at his side, is an open scroll, its parchment catching warm light from beyond the doorway. He is not reading it now; he already knows what it says.

Past the threshold, soft golden light spills outward from the inner courtyard, where a robed teacher figure — Christ, identifiable only by posture and the quality of the light around him, not by iconographic markers — stands with his back partly turned, as if having just spoken and now waiting. The light on that side of the frame is warm, alive, and slightly diffused, like light seen through incense smoke.

On the near side of the threshold, in the scribe's own courtyard, sits a stone altar. Its fire has gone out. A thin ribbon of cold smoke rises from dead coals — sacrifice offered, but no longer burning. This side of the frame should read cooler, stiller, more shadowed than the light beyond the door.

The composition is the whole theology: correct knowledge (the scroll), completed ritual (the altar, now cold), and a threshold not yet crossed. The scribe's posture should communicate genuine longing and hesitation rather than defiance — a man who answered rightly and now doesn't know how to close the remaining distance.

Visual language should feel premium, restrained, and theologically weighty — fine-art biblical realism, closer to a serious history painting than a movie poster. Avoid triumphalism, glowing CGI-spectacle, or sentimental religious-art cliché.

Composition: wide cinematic crop with negative space in the upper third for title text, the threshold line running roughly through the center of the frame, the scribe positioned slightly left of center on the shadowed side, the light and the teacher figure occupying the right third. Lighting should feel like the difference between studying a fire and standing in one.

## Negative Prompt

- no modern flags, political signage, or contemporary clothing
- no literal halos or glowing CGI light rays
- no triumphant or action-movie staging
- no cartoonish or overly stylized rendering
- no bright saturated colors
- no cluttered composition or crowd scenes — only the scribe and the distant teacher figure
- no obvious text embedded in the image
- no clear frontal view of Christ's face — keep him a figure of light and posture, not a portrait

## Brand Fit Check

- Recognizably Chukwuma Theology: serious, pastoral, contemporary in emotional intelligence, biblically grounded.
- Keeps the scene inside the biblical narrative — the threshold, the cold altar, the open scroll — rather than illustrating the modern Pew-survey hook from the essay's opening.
- Leaves clean negative space for a title treatment without losing the central image: a scroll in hand, a foot on the doorsill, light on the far side he hasn't yet stepped into.
