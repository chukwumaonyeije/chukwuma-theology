# Hero Image Prompt

## Primary Prompt

Create a cinematic editorial hero image for a theological essay titled **"When the Tank Is Not Low. It's Empty."** based on **1 Kings 19**.

Show an exhausted lone prophet figure in the wilderness beneath a sparse juniper tree at first light, not in melodramatic collapse but in truthful depletion. Nearby: simple bread baked on dark stones, a small jar of water, and the quiet aftermath of survival. The landscape should feel dry, still, and emotionally honest - muted desert earth, pale dawn gold, deep olive-gray shadows, restrained sky, no visual clutter.

The mood is pastoral, serious, and restorative rather than sensational. The image should communicate that God meets human exhaustion with gentle provision. Emphasize silence after intensity: the storm has passed, the world is still, and mercy has arrived in ordinary form.

Visual language should feel premium, contemplative, and theologically weighty - somewhere between fine-art photography and painterly realism. Avoid stock-photo burnout cliches. Avoid a generic businessman, hospital hallway, glowing fantasy angel, or triumphant revival aesthetic. This is not about hustle, collapse porn, or spectacle. It is about holy depletion, creaturely limits, and quiet divine care.

Composition: wide cinematic crop with negative space for title text, the prophet placed off-center, the bread and water visible enough to matter, the juniper tree providing fragile shelter, the wilderness stretching beyond him. Lighting should feel like mercy at dawn, not harsh noon or dramatic apocalypse.

## Style Keywords

- cinematic
- contemplative
- editorial
- painterly realism
- desert stillness
- sacred restraint
- emotional honesty
- dawn light
- theological gravitas

## Negative Prompt

- no modern office imagery
- no hospital monitors
- no glowing wings
- no excessive fantasy effects
- no smiling inspirational stock scene
- no sermon-stage theatrics
- no bright saturated colors
- no cluttered composition
- no obvious text embedded in image

## Brand Fit Check

- The image should feel recognizably Chukwuma Theology: serious, pastoral, contemporary in emotional intelligence, and biblically grounded.
- It should support a physician-theologian voice by feeling diagnostically honest rather than merely dramatic.
- It should leave room for a title treatment without losing the quiet central theology of the scene.
