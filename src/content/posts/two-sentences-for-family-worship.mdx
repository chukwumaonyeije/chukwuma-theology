*An Ebenezer sentence for what God has already done — and a second sentence for what is still unfinished.*

At our church retreat I asked families a simple question: *Where has God helped us?*

Later, writing [*Thus Far, But God*](/chukwuma-theology/posts/thus-far-but-god/), I realized we also need a second sentence — for the part of the story that is still unfinished.

Try both at your next family worship:

“I remember when God helped us by…”

“The place where I still need God’s help is…”

Let each person answer without requiring the second line to sound as confident as the first. A child may name something an adult missed. Someone may only be able to say, “I am still waiting.”

Pray both. Leave room for the person who can only manage the second.

Thus far the Lord has helped us.  
We can ask Him again today.
