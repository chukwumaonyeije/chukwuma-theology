Which Truth
Sets Us Free?

Pastor Kingsley Anonaba

Saturday sermon · John 8:31–32

TRUTH

John 8:31–32

“If you continue in My word, you are My disciples indeed. And you shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free.”

Freedom is not detached from discipleship. Jesus places knowing after continuing.

The central distinction

Facts can inform.
Truth must transform.

Biblical truth is not less than what is real. It is more: the faithful nature of God disclosed in His Word.

What remains when opinions shift?

Human judgment

Culture changes. Preferences compete. Authorities disagree. Even religious language can be made to serve us.

but

God’s self-revelation

“The word of our God stands forever.” Scripture remains the church’s norm because God does not change.

Isaiah 40:8

John 14:6

Truth has a face.

“I am the way, the truth, and the life.” The Christian answer is not finally a theory, a scholar, or a church office. It is Jesus Christ.

John 8:34

Freedom is not permission
to do whatever we want.

Freedom is the Spirit-given power to live according to God’s purpose.

Sin says, “You must obey me.” Jesus says, “You are free to obey God.”

The sermon’s movement

The Word does not stop at the mind.

WordKnowledgeFaithObedienceFreedom

What truth looks like in a believer

Truth leaves evidence.

Honesty

Falsehood loses its hiding place.

Scripture-shaped judgment

The Word becomes lamp and light.

Faith in Christ

Truth is received, not merely admired.

Obedience

Love for Jesus becomes visible.

Integrity

Speech, work, and relationships agree.

Sanctification

The Spirit continues the work of holiness.

An interior turn

Where does my confession of truth contradict my practice?

Truth is not something we possess while protecting the lie we refuse to confess.

Practice, not abstraction

Live the truth you have received.

Reflect · discuss · pray

What would “continuing in My word” change in your life this week?

A habit to reorder. A lie to confess. A command to obey. A relationship to make honest.

The closing summons

Truth sets us free when the Christ we confess becomes the life we practice.

Remain in His Word. Receive His truth. Walk in His freedom.
