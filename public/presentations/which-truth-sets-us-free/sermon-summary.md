# Which Truth Sets Us Free?

**Preached by Pastor Kingsley Anonaba for the Grace Eternal Prayer Meeting on Saturday, August 22, 2026.**

## Sermon summary

Beginning with Jesus’ words in John 8:31–32, the sermon asks a sharper question than “What is truth?”: **Which truth actually makes a human being free?** Its answer is that biblical truth is not merely accurate information or a conclusion reached by human reasoning. Truth is grounded in the unchanging nature of God, revealed in Scripture, and personally embodied in Jesus Christ.

The sermon contrasts this absolute truth with claims shaped by culture, preference, philosophy, or religious tradition. Human opinions change; God’s Word endures. The Christian standard is therefore not the charisma of a preacher, the prestige of a scholar, or the customs of a community. Christ is the standard. To know truth is to know Him, remain in His Word, and allow that Word to reshape the life.

This makes freedom more than intellectual enlightenment. According to the sermon, sin is bondage, and the freedom Jesus gives is the power to live according to God’s purpose. It is not permission to do whatever we want. The movement is practical: the Word gives knowledge; knowledge awakens faith; faith leads to obedience; and obedience becomes the lived expression of freedom.

Truth therefore leaves visible evidence. It produces honesty instead of deception, integrity in relationships and work, obedience to God’s commands, and continuing sanctification through the Holy Spirit. A person cannot claim to possess truth while deliberately practicing falsehood. Truth must move from doctrine into speech, choices, habits, and relationships.

The sermon closes with a call to study Scripture daily, receive Jesus personally, obey God’s Word, reject deception, confess and forsake sin, follow the Holy Spirit, practice honesty, remain in Christ, and teach truth to others. The central burden is simple and searching: **truth sets us free only when the Christ we confess becomes the life we practice.**
