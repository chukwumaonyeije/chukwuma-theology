# The Crossroads of Destiny

<div class="subtitle">The Reset You Didn't Know You Needed</div>

<div class="accent-line"></div>

<p class="reference">Key Scripture: Luke 5:1–11</p>

<p class="credit">Sermon preached by Pastor Evan Knott — May 30, 2026</p>

---

<!-- _class: split -->

<div>
  <div class="eyebrow">The Opening Story</div>
  <h2>When Every Road Leads to Tragedy</h2>
  <div class="accent-line"></div>
</div>

<div class="panel">
  <ul>
    <li>O. Henry wrote a chilling story called <strong>"Roads of Destiny"</strong>.</li>
    <li>A young poet named David leaves home and arrives at a crossroads:
      <ul>
        <li>Goes right — and dies.</li>
        <li>Goes left — and dies.</li>
        <li>Turns back — and dies.</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    <li>Every path meets the same fatal pistol.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

---

<div class="eyebrow">The Persistent Dread</div>

## The Trap of Tragic Fate

<div class="accent-line"></div>

<p class="wide">We shiver at O. Henry's story because it voices our deepest dread: that our choices don't matter, and we are merely victims of fate.</p>

<div class="grid two">
  <div class="card">
    <h3>The Human Condition</h3>
    <p>Feeling trapped in loops of hopelessness, exhaustion, or cynicism.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card alt">
    <h3>Simon Peter's Boat</h3>
    <p>Felt that same dread after a fruitless, exhausting night of catching absolutely nothing on the lake.</p>
  </div>
</div>

---

<!-- _class: quote -->

<div class="eyebrow">The Way of the Water</div>

<div class="statement">Jesus broke away from the crowd that morning to pull Peter out of his cynicism.</div>

<p class="dim">He showed him, and us, that there is another way at the crossroads. It is the way of the water.</p>

---

<!-- _class: split -->

<div>
  <div class="eyebrow">Luke 5:1–2</div>
  <h2>The Divine Interruption</h2>
  <div class="accent-line"></div>
</div>

<div class="panel soft">
  <p><em>"Once, while Jesus was standing beside the lake of Gennesaret and the crowd was pressing in on him to hear the word of God, he saw two boats there at the shore of the lake."</em></p>
  <ul>
    <li>Jesus steps into our everyday workplace and routine.</li>
    <li>The shore is where our ordinary lives meet His extraordinary calling.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

---

<!-- _class: light -->

<div class="eyebrow">The Path of Discipleship</div>

# Five Lessons from Luke 5

<div class="accent-line"></div>

<ul>
  <li>Discipleship is a journey of resets and surprises.</li>
  <li>It transforms tired professionals into fishers of men.</li>
  <li>Let's examine how Jesus rewrites our "tragic destiny."</li>
</ul>

---

<!-- _class: split -->

<div>
  <div class="eyebrow">Lesson I</div>
  <h2>Jesus Always Makes the First Move</h2>
  <div class="accent-line"></div>
  <p class="reference">Luke 5:3</p>
</div>

<div class="panel">
  <ul>
    <li>Peter didn't volunteer; he was just cleaning his nets.</li>
    <li>Jesus spotted him, initiated, and asked for a favor.</li>
    <li><strong>Key Truth:</strong> The road to following Jesus always begins from somewhere outside of ourselves. God initiates grace.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

---

<!-- _class: split -->

<div>
  <div class="eyebrow">Lesson II</div>
  <h2>First Steps Are Always Near the Shore</h2>
  <div class="accent-line"></div>
  <p class="reference">Luke 5:3–4</p>
</div>

<div class="panel soft">
  <ul>
    <li>Jesus only asked Peter to put out <em>a little</em> from the land.</li>
    <li>In the shallow waters, all Peter had to do was listen.</li>
    <li><strong>Key Truth:</strong> Discipleship doesn't demand you jump into the deep end right away. Start simple.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

---

<!-- _class: split -->

<div>
  <div class="eyebrow">Lesson III</div>
  <h2>Discipleship Is for Everyone</h2>
  <div class="accent-line"></div>
  <p class="reference">Luke 5:1–11</p>
</div>

<div class="panel">
  <ul>
    <li>Jesus reaches out to lepers, Pharisees, tax collectors, and tired fishermen.</li>
    <li>He bypasses the religious elite to select ordinary working people.</li>
    <li><strong>Key Truth:</strong> He is looking for you, specifically. Discipleship is not for someone else.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

---

<!-- _class: split -->

<div>
  <div class="eyebrow">Lesson IV</div>
  <h2>Keep Following into Deeper Water</h2>
  <div class="accent-line"></div>
  <p class="reference">Luke 5:4</p>
</div>

<div class="panel soft">
  <ul>
    <li>Jesus didn't leave Peter in the shallows. He called him to <strong>"put out into the deep."</strong></li>
    <li>The deep water is where the miracle is hidden.</li>
    <li><strong>Key Truth:</strong> Jesus sees the joy, the purpose, and the fish in your future that you cannot yet see.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

---

<!-- _class: split -->

<div>
  <div class="eyebrow">Lesson V</div>
  <h2>Keep Jesus in Your Boat</h2>
  <div class="accent-line"></div>
  <p class="reference">Luke 5:8–11</p>
</div>

<div class="panel">
  <ul>
    <li>When Peter saw the catch, he fell at Jesus' knees.</li>
    <li>His words said <em>"depart from me, for I am a sinful man,"</em> but his heart cried <em>"don't leave me."</em></li>
    <li><strong>Key Truth:</strong> Never strike out on your own without Him. True safety is in His presence.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

---

<div class="eyebrow">Practical Application</div>

## Step 1: Listen Near the Shore

<div class="accent-line"></div>

<p class="wide">Before the deep water, there must be listening in the shallows.</p>

<div class="grid two">
  <div class="card">
    <h3>The Shallows</h3>
    <p>Spend 15 minutes in quiet reflection, reading a Gospel, or talking honestly with God.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card alt">
    <h3>The posture</h3>
    <p>Be still enough to hear His voice over the noise of your doubts and frustrations.</p>
  </div>
</div>

---

<!-- _class: quote -->

<div class="eyebrow">Practical Application</div>

<div class="statement">Step 2: Put Out Into the Deep</div>

<p>Take the risk of obedience even when it doesn't make sense to your experienced mind.</p>

<p class="dim">When Jesus says "cast your nets," trust Him over your past failures.</p>

---

<div class="eyebrow">Practical Application</div>

## Step 3: Face Your Fears & Unworthiness

<div class="accent-line"></div>

<ul>
  <li>Peter was overwhelmed by his own inadequacy: <em>"I am a sinful man."</em></li>
  <li>Jesus didn't deny Peter's sin; He redirected his future: <em>"Do not be afraid; from now on you will be catching people."</em></li>
  <li>Your past does not disqualify you from His purpose.</li>
</ul>

---

<!-- _class: quote -->

<div class="eyebrow">Practical Application</div>

<div class="statement">Step 4: Leave the Nets Behind</div>

<p>When they brought their boats to land, they left everything and followed Him.</p>

<p class="dim">What security, routine, or control are you holding onto that keeps you on the shore?</p>

---

<!-- _class: split -->

<div>
  <div class="eyebrow">The Resolution</div>
  <h2>Grace at the Crossroads</h2>
  <div class="accent-line"></div>
</div>

<div class="panel">
  <ul>
    <li>We are not victims of a Marquis's pistol or random fate.</li>
    <li>At our crossroads, there is a Savior who meets us, boards our boat, and guides us.</li>
    <li>The empty nets of the night are replaced by the overflowing nets of His grace.</li>
  </ul>
</div>
