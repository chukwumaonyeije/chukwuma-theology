# Real Examples — Atlanta North SDA Church

Source: Dr. Onyeije's actual delivered invocations and offering calls, 2023–2025 (from "Elder Duties - Invocation, Offering, Prayer" archive). Study these for voice, pacing, and structure before drafting new pieces. Do not copy phrases verbatim into new pieces — the point is to internalize the pattern, not reuse the wording.

## Invocation Examples

### Example 1 — Invocation, July 15, 2023

> Our Loving Heavenly Father,
>
> As we gather on this blessed Sabbath day, we come before Your presence with hearts filled with reverence and gratitude.
> We invoke Your Holy Spirit to descend upon us, filling this sanctuary with Your divine presence and grace.
>
> Oh Lord, we acknowledge You as the Creator of the heavens and the earth, the Author of our salvation, and the One who upholds us in every moment.
> We are humbled by Your infinite love & mercy that has brought us together on this Sabbath Day to follow Your commandments and embrace Your truth.
>
> On this Sabbath day, we seek Your presence to guide us in our worship, study, and fellowship.
> Pour out Your blessings upon us as we delve into Your Word, that we may gain a deeper understanding of Your truth and be transformed by its power.
> Open our hearts to receive the messages You have prepared for us, that we may be inspired to live lives that reflect Your character.
>
> Heavenly Father, we ask for Your wisdom and discernment as we navigate the challenges and temptations of this world. Help us to be faithful witnesses of Your love, compassion, and grace in our interactions with others. Empower us to share the blessed hope of Your soon return, that more may come to know and accept Your saving grace.
>
> We lift up our church leaders before You, dear Lord. Grant them wisdom, strength, and humility as they shepherd Your flock. Fill them with Your Holy Spirit, that they may lead us in paths of righteousness, unity, and spiritual growth.
>
> As we come together as a church family, we also remember those in need within our community and beyond. We ask for Your compassion and healing to be upon the sick, the lonely, and the broken-hearted.
>
> Gracious Lord, we are grateful for the privilege of worshiping You freely and for the Sabbath rest that allows us to deepen our connection with You. May this Sabbath day be a time of spiritual refreshing and renewal, drawing us closer to You and to one another.
>
> We offer this prayer with hearts full of praise and thanksgiving, knowing that You are faithful to hear and answer our petitions according to Your perfect will.
>
> In the name of Jesus Christ, our Savior and Redeemer, we pray. Amen.
>
> **OUR OPENING HYMN THIS MORNING IS: Hymn # 341 — To God Be the Glory**

### Example 2 — Invocation, January 13, 2024

> Heavenly Father,
>
> As we gather today in Your holy presence, we welcome You among us with hearts full of joy and gratitude. We celebrate the gift of life You have bestowed upon us and humbly ask for the openness to hear Your voice. May our spirits be attuned to Your wisdom, guided by Your hand, and enveloped in Your boundless love.
>
> Lord, we stand in awe of Your magnificent creation, the intricate beauty and perfection of Your work that surrounds us. In every leaf, every drop of water, and every breath of air, we see Your handiwork. We are honored and humbled to be called Your children, to be part of Your grand design.
>
> Today, we pray to be true worshipers, not just in our songs and words but in our every action and thought. Help us to live a life of continuous connection with You, recognizing Your presence in all our daily activities. May our lives be a reflection of Your love and grace, spreading Your light wherever we go.
>
> In Your holy name, we pray. Amen.

### Example 3 — Invocation Prayer, April 13, 2024 (scripture-anchored, end-time theme)

> Dear Heavenly Father,
>
> We come before You today with reverence and gratitude, seeking Your divine presence and guidance in our midst. Your Word reveals the depths of Your wisdom and the boundless nature of Your love, and we stand in awe of Your greatness.
>
> As we gather, we are reminded of the powerful message in Revelation 5:12, which proclaims, "Worthy is the Lamb who was slain, to receive power and wealth and wisdom and might and honor and glory and blessing!" We thank You for the ultimate sacrifice Jesus made on the cross and the triumph He secured over sin and death.
>
> We lift up our church leaders and members to You, asking that You fill them with Your love, wisdom, and discernment. Grant them the courage to remain steadfast in their faith and to be a beacon of hope in a world that often seems shrouded in darkness.
>
> Lord, as we navigate these challenging times, we recognize the signs of Your imminent return. Help us to keep our eyes firmly fixed on You, and give us the strength to remain faithful witnesses of Your grace and love.
>
> We pray all these things in the precious name of Jesus, our Worthy Lamb. Amen.

## Call for Offering Examples

### Example 1 — Offering, July 15, 2023 (missionary illustration)

> It is time for Tithes and Offering:
>
> How would you feel if everyone in your neighborhood chose to put their trash in your front yard every evening when you went to sleep.
>
> I read the story of a Christian School in a Predominantly Muslim Middle Eastern country. The church school was set up by the General Conference... Few attended the school, and the locals felt that the presence of a Christian school was a disgrace to them. They expressed their displeasure by dumping their garbage on the school grounds every night without fail. And, just as reliably, the missionary would emerge every morning and clean up the garbage.
>
> Our missionary friend trusted that Christ's model of mission — humbling Himself to come among His children and experience suffering and shame — would somehow trigger a breakthrough. When interviewed afterwards the missionary quoted Philippians 2:5–8...
>
> The result: what was previously unthinkable happened. The local people were moved by the foreigner who was silently — without remonstration — disposing of their garbage. Eventually, the villagers ceased dumping at the school and even entrusted their kids to the missionaries.
>
> Often, the greatest barrier in mission is not within the people we want to reach, but our failure to properly embody God's compassion and generosity.
>
> [theological bridge to giving as sacrifice, tied to Christ's own self-giving]
>
> **THREE WAYS OF GIVING:**
> 1. Adventist Giving website — accessed via ANC website, QR Code, or text "GIVE" to 770-818-4323
> 2. SDA Giving App (Android & iPhone)
> 3. Offering Box in the Foyer
>
> **PRAYER FOR TITHES AND OFFERINGS**
>
> Dear Heavenly Father,
>
> We come before You today with hearts filled with gratitude and reverence. We thank You for the gift of Jesus Christ, who entered a world separated from You because of sin, to reconcile humanity to Yourself. Through His sacrificial death on the cross, He offered Himself as the purest and most precious offering.
>
> Father, we ask that You shape our hearts and motives when it comes to giving. Help us understand that it is not merely about the amount we give but the attitude and intention behind our offerings. May our giving flow from a heart overflowing with love and devotion for You.
>
> In Jesus' name, we pray. Amen.

### Example 2 — Offering, January 13, 2024 (personal-story illustration, direct address to congregation)

> "Dear members of our congregation, [Pause for effect] Today, we come together to carry out an important and joyful act of worship — the giving of our tithes and offerings.
>
> I want to share a brief story with you that underscores the beauty of giving. There was a young boy in a congregation, let's call him Timothy, who received a small amount of money for his birthday. It wasn't much, but to him, it was a treasure. The following Sabbath, during our tithes and offerings, Timothy came forward with an envelope... inside was all his birthday money. When his mother asked him why he gave it all, Timothy said, 'Because God has given me everything — my family, my friends, and He even gave me this birthday money. I just wanted to give something back to Him.'
>
> [Pause and smile] Timothy's simple act of faith and generosity reminds us that when we give, we are not just fulfilling a duty; we are expressing our heartfelt gratitude to God for His endless blessings."
>
> [Slow down and emphasize] Leviticus 27:30 says: 'All the tithe of the land... is the Lord's: it is holy unto the Lord.'
>
> [Pause and look around the congregation] Tithing is not merely a duty, but a testament of our faith and recognition that everything we have is a blessing from God.
>
> [Pause and smile] There are several ways for you to give today. You may choose to contribute through our website, via our mobile app, by sending a text message, or by placing your gift in our offering boxes located in the foyer.
>
> [Pause, then speak with sincerity] As we give, let us pray, 'Dear Lord, we thank You for Your provision and the opportunity to return a portion of what You have blessed us with. We pray for wisdom and guidance in the use of these funds for the advancement of Your Kingdom. Amen.'
>
> [Pause, then conclude warmly] Thank you for your generous hearts. God bless you all."

### Example 3 — Tithes and Offering, April 8, 2023 (personal testimony illustration, from Dr. Onyeije's own life)

> There was once a time when I thought that Tithing was all about money. Tithing is not just about giving money, it actually reflects our trust and faith in God. By giving our tithes, we acknowledge that God is the source of all our blessings.
>
> I would like to share a personal story of how tithing has impacted my life. I have only had five jobs in my life: paper boy at age 12, camp counselor, tutor in college, pizza delivery for Domino's, and as a physician. As a young physician, I would always pay my Tithe but struggled with financial stability... One Sabbath, Chichi and I went to a seminar about tithing at church and we decided to not pay our tithe on our net but on our gross. I soon began to see a change in my financial situation... But what impacted me the most was the sense of peace and security I felt knowing that I was putting God first in my finances.
>
> Ellen G. White wrote extensively on tithing. In "Testimonies for the Church, Volume 3," she tells of a farming family who faithfully tithed despite hardship, and whose field alone was spared in a hailstorm that destroyed their neighbors' crops.
>
> So, my dear friends, I encourage you to consider the importance of tithing in your own life.

## Notes on the pattern across all examples

- **Personal illustrations are common and effective.** Dr. Onyeije draws on his own biography (five jobs, marriage, tithing struggles) as often as borrowed stories. New pieces should feel free to invent a plausible, well-worn illustration (missionary story, congregation-member story, parable-style anecdote) in the same register — vivid, concrete, a little slow-building, with a clear turn toward the giving theme.
- **Scripture is load-bearing, not decorative.** One or two references, quoted in full, placed where they land hardest — usually right after the illustration or at the theological pivot.
- **Ellen G. White is an occasional guest, not a requirement.** Cite a specific book/volume/page when used, in the same way a preacher would cite a commentary.
- **The three giving methods are a fixed, reusable block** for ANC — reproduce them near-verbatim (see SKILL.md).
- **Stage directions in brackets** (`[Pause for effect]`, `[Slow down and emphasize]`) appear in the more performative offering calls, not in the invocations. Use them when the piece reads as something to be spoken aloud with dramatic pacing; skip them for shorter, more contemplative pieces.
