# RESOURCES.md Format

`RESOURCES.md` lives at `./great-controversy/RESOURCES.md`. It is the curated set of trusted sources for this study. Knowledge in lessons should be drawn from here — not from parametric guesses.

## Structure

```md
# The Great Controversy — Resources

## Primary Sources

- [*The Great Controversy* — Ellen G. White (Ellen White Estate)](https://egwwritings.org/book/b132)
  The text itself. All lessons cite chapter and paragraph numbers from this edition.
- [Ellen White Writings (egwwritings.org)](https://egwwritings.org)
  Full corpus searchable. Use for cross-referencing GC themes in *Desire of Ages*, *Patriarchs and Prophets*, *Acts of the Apostles*, etc.

## Historical & Theological Reference

- [*Daniel and the Revelation* — Uriah Smith](https://egwwritings.org/book/b137)
  Classic SDA exposition of the prophetic chapters underlying Part III–IV of the GC.
- [Andrews University Seminary (Andrews.edu)](https://www.andrews.edu/sem/)
  Academic SDA scholarship. Use for deep prophetic and historical questions.
- [Adventist Biblical Research Institute (biblicalresearch.gc.adventist.org)](https://biblicalresearch.gc.adventist.org)
  Peer-reviewed SDA biblical scholarship. High-trust for doctrinal questions.

## Historical Background

- [*The Reformation* — Owen Chadwick]
  Context for Part I–II of the GC. Secular historian, high-trust on facts.
- [Oxford Dictionary of Church History]
  For verifying claims about popes, councils, reformers.

## Communities / Wisdom

- Sabbath School class / Elder board — real-time discussion and application
- [Adventist Review (adventistreview.org)](https://www.adventistreview.org) — pastoral and contemporary application
- [Ministry Magazine (ministrymagazine.org)](https://www.ministrymagazine.org) — pastoral preaching angle
- [Ellen White Estate Research Center](https://ellenwhite.org) — authoritative EGW questions

## Gaps

{List any areas where no good resource has been found yet — drives future search.}
```

## Rules

- **Primary sources first.** The GC text itself, plus Scripture, are always the anchor.
- **Annotate every entry.** One line: what it covers and when to reach for it.
- **High-trust only.** No paraphrase sites, no secondary summaries masquerading as primary.
- **Prune ruthlessly.** A resource found inaccurate or shallow gets removed.
