# Learning Record Format

Learning records live in `./great-controversy/learning-records/` and use sequential numbering: `0001-slug.md`, `0002-slug.md`, etc.

They capture non-obvious insights, demonstrated understanding, and corrected misconceptions — the teaching equivalent of ADRs. Used to calculate the zone of proximal development.

## Template

```md
# {Short title — what was learned or established}

{1–3 sentences: what was understood (or what prior knowledge was established), and why it matters for future sessions. Be specific about the GC chapter or doctrine involved.}
```

That is the whole format. Most records are a single paragraph.

## Optional sections

Only include when they add real value:

- **Status** — `active | superseded by LR-NNNN`
- **Evidence** — how he demonstrated understanding (question answered, exercise completed, prior knowledge cited)
- **Implications** — what this unlocks for future sessions

## When to write a learning record

1. **He demonstrated genuine understanding of something non-trivial** — not just exposure. He can explain it, apply it, or use it correctly in a new context.
2. **He disclosed prior knowledge** — "I already know Luther's role in the Reformation." Record the depth claimed.
3. **A misconception was corrected** — e.g., he thought the investigative judgment began at Christ's ascension; now he understands 1844.
4. **The mission shifted** — he discovered a different goal as the study developed.

### What does NOT qualify
- A chapter was covered in a lesson. Coverage ≠ learning.
- Something already defined in the glossary as a bare term.
- Session-by-session activity logs.

## Supersession

When a later record corrects an earlier one, mark the old record `Status: superseded by LR-NNNN`. Don't delete — the arc of understanding is itself useful signal.
