# MISSION.md Format

`MISSION.md` lives at `./great-controversy/MISSION.md`. It captures *why* Chukwuma is studying *The Great Controversy* — the real-world outcome he's after. Every teaching decision traces back here.

## Template

```md
# Mission: The Great Controversy

## Why
{1–3 sentences. The concrete goal. What changes in his preaching, teaching, writing, or conviction when he finishes this study? Avoid "to understand the book" — push for the underlying outcome.}

## Success looks like
- {A specific, observable thing he will be able to do — e.g., "Preach a sermon series on the sanctuary from the GC framework"}
- {Another specific outcome}
- {…}

## Reading approach
{Is he reading the book chapter-by-chapter alongside lessons? Or relying on lessons to cover the content? What's his pace?}

## Prior knowledge
{What does he already know well — specific chapters, doctrines, historical periods? What can we skip or move through quickly?}

## Constraints
- {Time per session, frequency, any parts of the book he wants to skip or deprioritize}

## Out of scope
- {Adjacent topics explicitly not the focus right now}
```

## Rules

- **Concrete over abstract.** "Preach a 5-part GC series at my church by Advent" beats "understand the great controversy better."
- **Push back on vagueness.** If he can't articulate a real goal, interview him before writing anything.
- **Revise when reality shifts.** Update this file as his goals clarify. Don't let a stale mission steer future sessions.
- **One file.** All GC study lives in this one mission.
