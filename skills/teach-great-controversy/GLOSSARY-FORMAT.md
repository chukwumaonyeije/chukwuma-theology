# Glossary Format

The glossary lives at `./great-controversy/reference/glossary.html`. It is the canonical theological vocabulary for this study. All lessons and learning records should use its terminology.

## Structure

The HTML glossary should be beautiful and printable. Internally, terms follow this pattern:

```
Great Controversy Theme:
The cosmic conflict between Christ and Satan — played out in heaven, in history, and in the human heart — that provides Ellen White's interpretive lens for all of Scripture and church history.
Avoid: spiritual warfare (too narrow), cosmic drama (too vague)

Investigative Judgment:
The pre-Advent judgment beginning in 1844 (end of the 2300-day prophecy of Daniel 8:14) in which the cases of the dead are reviewed in heaven before Christ's return.
Avoid: pre-Advent judgment (acceptable synonym), final judgment (conflates with the executive judgment)

Sanctuary Doctrine:
The Adventist teaching that Christ entered the Most Holy Place of the heavenly sanctuary in 1844 to begin the final phase of His high-priestly ministry, corresponding to the Day of Atonement.
Avoid: heavenly temple doctrine (imprecise)

Remnant Church:
The end-time community called to keep the commandments of God and hold the testimony of Jesus (Rev. 12:17), identified in Adventism with the SDA movement.
Avoid: true church (triumphalist), last-day saints (informal)
```

## Rules

- **Add a term only when he understands it** — not when it's first introduced. The glossary records compressed knowledge, not preview vocabulary.
- **Be opinionated.** Pick the best term; list synonyms to avoid.
- **Keep definitions tight.** One to two sentences. What the term IS, not what it does.
- **Use the glossary's own terms inside definitions.** Once "great controversy theme" is defined, use it in other definitions.
- **Flag ambiguities.** If a term is used loosely in the wider Adventist world, note the working resolution for this study.
- **Revise as understanding deepens.** A week-one definition may be imprecise by week six. Update in place.
